MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into between Vireo Labs Inc.
and the counterparty identified below, for the sole purpose of facilitating
discussions regarding a potential business relationship (the "Purpose").

1. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by
either party to the other in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed
orally, in writing, or in any other form.

2. Obligations
Each party agrees to use the other party's Confidential Information solely
for the Purpose and to protect it using the same degree of care it uses to
protect its own confidential information of a similar nature.

3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or
becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party; (b) was
rightfully known to the receiving party prior to disclosure; or (c) is
independently developed without use of the disclosing party's Confidential
Information.

4. No License
Nothing in this Agreement grants either party any license or other right to
the other party's Confidential Information beyond the limited purpose of
evaluating the Purpose.

5. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
