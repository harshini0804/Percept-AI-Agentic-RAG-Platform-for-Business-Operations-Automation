MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is entered into between Quilyx Analytics LLC
("Provider") and the Client identified below.

1. Scope of Services
Provider shall furnish data analytics services as described in one or more
Statements of Work executed by the parties from time to time.

2. Performance Standards
Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the performance
benchmarks generally expected for services of this nature, taking into
account prevailing industry conditions at the relevant time. Where Provider's
performance is found to have materially and repeatedly fallen short of such
expectations, appropriate adjustments may be considered by mutual agreement,
having regard to the circumstances then prevailing and the extent to which
any shortfall was attributable to factors outside Provider's reasonable
control.

3. Confidentiality
Both parties agree to protect Confidential Information disclosed under this
Agreement using at least the same degree of care used to protect their own
confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

4. Term
This Agreement remains in effect until terminated by either party in
accordance with the applicable Statement of Work.

5. Dispute Resolution
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be referred to the
parties' respective account managers for good-faith resolution before either
party pursues formal proceedings.
