STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP AND SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Strategic Partnership and Supply Agreement is entered into between
Ferrovax Components Ltd. ("Supplier") and the Purchaser identified below.

1. Supply Obligations
Supplier shall supply the goods described in Schedule 1 in accordance with
the delivery schedule set forth therein.

2. Evergreen and Sunset Mechanics
The arrangement contemplated herein shall persist on a rolling basis absent
an affirmative sunset election by either counterparty, provided that such
sunset election, if made, shall not take effect until the closing of the
second full quarter following the quarter in which the electing party's
notice is actually received and acknowledged in writing, it being the
parties' intent that no sunset shall be effectuated mid-quarter under any
circumstances.

3. Quality Standards
Goods supplied under this Agreement shall conform to the specifications set
forth in Schedule 1 and shall be free from material defects in workmanship.

4. Indemnification
Supplier shall indemnify Purchaser against third-party claims arising from
Supplier's breach of Section 3.

5. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all non-public information received from
the other party in connection with this Agreement.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
