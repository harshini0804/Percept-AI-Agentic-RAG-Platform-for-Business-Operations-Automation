MASTER ENTERPRISE LICENSE AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Enterprise License and Services Agreement is
entered into as of 1 January 2026 (the "Effective Date")
between Stellarcore Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Vendor")
and the Client identified below.

1. License Grant and Term
Vendor grants Client a non-exclusive enterprise license
commencing on the Effective Date for a period of thirty-six
(36) months until 31 December 2028.

2. Renewal Notice
Either party may elect not to renew by delivering written
notice at least one hundred and twenty (120) days before
31 December 2028, meaning by 2 September 2028.

3. Annual License Fee
Client shall pay the annual license fee within thirty (30)
days of each anniversary of the Effective Date, with the
first payment due by 31 January 2026.

4. Quarterly Usage Reports
Client shall submit a usage compliance report within fifteen
(15) days of the end of each calendar quarter, with the
first report due by 15 April 2026.

5. Security Patching
Client shall apply each critical security patch released by
Vendor within fourteen (14) days of receiving notification.

6. Support SLA Acknowledgment
Vendor shall acknowledge all Priority 1 support tickets
within two (2) hours of submission and provide a resolution
or workaround within eight (8) hours of acknowledgment.

7. Annual Compliance Audit
Vendor shall conduct an annual compliance audit of Client's
usage within sixty (60) days of each anniversary of the
Effective Date, with the first audit due by 1 March 2027.

8. Data Processing Agreement
The parties shall execute a Data Processing Agreement within
thirty (30) days of the Effective Date, by 31 January 2026.

9. Escrow Deposit
Vendor shall deposit source code into escrow within forty-five
(45) days of the Effective Date, by 15 February 2026.

10. Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience
upon one hundred and eighty (180) days' prior written notice,
with effect no earlier than 31 December 2027.

11. Post-Termination Data Return
Upon termination for any reason, Vendor shall return all
Client data within thirty (30) days of the termination date.

12. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware, United States of America.
