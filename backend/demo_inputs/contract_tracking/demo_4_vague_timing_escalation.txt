MANAGEMENT CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Management Consulting Services Agreement is entered into
between Thornfield Advisory LLP ("Consultant") and the Client.

1. Deliverable Timelines
Consultant shall deliver each advisory report within a
reasonable time following the relevant briefing, taking into
account the complexity of the subject matter and any
information availability constraints at the relevant time.

2. Stakeholder Engagement
Consultant shall engage with Client's key stakeholders at
such times and in such formats as are appropriate given the
nature of the engagement and the parties' mutual availability.

3. Implementation Support
Following delivery of each report, Consultant shall provide
reasonable implementation support to Client as and when
required, for such duration as the parties consider necessary
in the circumstances.

4. Progress Reporting
Consultant shall provide progress updates to Client's
management team at intervals that reflect the pace of the
engagement and the information needs of Client's leadership
at the relevant time.

5. Knowledge Transfer
Upon completion of the engagement, Consultant shall conduct
knowledge transfer activities at a time and in a manner
to be agreed between the parties, having regard to the
complexity of the subject matter and Client's readiness.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
