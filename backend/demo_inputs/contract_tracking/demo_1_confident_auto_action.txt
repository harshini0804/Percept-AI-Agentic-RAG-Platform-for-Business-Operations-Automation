SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This SaaS Subscription Agreement is entered into between Nexora
Cloud Inc. ("Provider") and the Client identified below.

1. Subscription Term
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for
twelve (12) months. It renews automatically for successive twelve
(12) month terms unless either party provides written notice of
non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the
then-current term.

2. Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon
thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.

3. Payment Terms
Client shall pay the annual subscription fee within fourteen (14)
days of the invoice date. Invoices unpaid after fourteen (14) days
shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

4. Data Deletion on Termination
Upon termination or expiry of this Agreement, Provider shall
permanently delete all Client data within thirty (30) days of the
termination date, and provide written confirmation of deletion upon
Client's request.

5. Intellectual Property
All intellectual property rights in the Provider's platform remain
with Provider. Client receives a limited, non-exclusive license to
use the platform during the Subscription Term only.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
