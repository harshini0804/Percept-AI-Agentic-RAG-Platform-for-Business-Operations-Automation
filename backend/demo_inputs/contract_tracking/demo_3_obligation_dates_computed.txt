TECHNOLOGY PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This Technology Partnership Agreement is entered into as of
1 February 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Axonix
Technology Partners Ltd. ("Partner A") and the co-partner
identified below.

1. Initial Term and Renewal
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues
for twelve (12) months. It renews automatically for successive
twelve (12) month periods unless either party delivers written
notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end
of the then-current term.

2. Quarterly Business Review
Each party shall prepare and share a business review report
within fifteen (15) days of the end of each calendar quarter,
with the first report due by 15 April 2026.

3. Joint Roadmap Submission
The parties shall jointly submit a technology roadmap to their
respective steering committees within forty-five (45) days of
the Effective Date, meaning by 17 March 2026.

4. Data Sharing Protocol
Partner A shall implement the agreed data sharing protocol
within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date, by 3 March 2026.

5. Annual Security Review
Each party shall conduct a joint security review within sixty
(60) days of each anniversary of the Effective Date, with the
first review due by 1 April 2027.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
