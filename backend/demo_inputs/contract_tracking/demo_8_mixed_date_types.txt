ENTERPRISE SERVICES AND SUPPORT AGREEMENT

This Enterprise Services and Support Agreement is entered
into as of 1 April 2026 (the "Effective Date") between
Coronal Systems Ltd. ("Provider") and the Client below.

1. Term and Renewal
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues
for twenty-four (24) months. It renews automatically unless
either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least
ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

2. Implementation Timeline
Provider shall complete the initial implementation within
sixty (60) days of the Effective Date, by 31 May 2026.

3. Monthly Service Reports
Provider shall deliver a service performance report within
five (5) business days of the end of each calendar month,
with the first report due by 8 May 2026.

4. Incident Response
Upon receiving a Priority 1 incident report from Client,
Provider shall acknowledge within one (1) hour and provide
a resolution or workaround within four (4) hours of the
initial notification.

5. Fee Review
Provider may review and adjust the service fee from time
to time to reflect changes in the cost of service delivery,
with any adjustment to take effect at a mutually convenient
point following reasonable advance notice to Client.

6. Data Security Standards
Provider shall maintain ISO 27001 certification or an
equivalent recognised standard throughout the term of this
Agreement and shall never allow its certification to lapse.

7. Escalation Procedure
In the event of a service dispute that cannot be resolved
at the account management level, either party may escalate
the matter to senior leadership, who shall engage in good
faith resolution efforts within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.
