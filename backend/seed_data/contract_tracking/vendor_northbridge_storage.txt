VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between
Northbridge Cloud Storage Inc. ("Vendor") and the Client identified in the
signature block below.

1. Term and Renewal
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period
of twelve (12) months. Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew
for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party provides written
notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-
current term.

2. Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90)
days' prior written notice to the other party.

3. Confidentiality
Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other party's
Confidential Information and shall not disclose such information to any
third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.

4. Fees and Payment
Client shall pay Vendor the fees set forth in Exhibit A within thirty (30)
days of the invoice date. Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of
1.5% per month.

5. Limitation of Liability
Neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, or
consequential damages arising out of or related to this Agreement.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws
of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
