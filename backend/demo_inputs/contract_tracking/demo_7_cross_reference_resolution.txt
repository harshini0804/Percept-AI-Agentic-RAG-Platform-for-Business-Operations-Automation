SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT AND LICENSING AGREEMENT

This Software Development and Licensing Agreement is entered
into as of 15 March 2026 (the "Effective Date") between
Praxis Software Labs Inc. ("Developer") and Client below.

1. Definitions
"Delivery Schedule" means the milestone dates set out in
Exhibit A attached hereto, as may be amended by written
agreement. "Warranty Period" means ninety (90) days
following each Delivery Milestone.

2. Development Milestones
Developer shall complete each milestone in accordance with
the Delivery Schedule defined in Section 1 and shall notify
Client in writing upon completion of each milestone.

3. Warranty and Bug Fixes
During the Warranty Period as defined in Section 1,
Developer shall remedy any material defect in the delivered
software within fourteen (14) days of receiving written
notice from Client.

4. Payment Terms
Client shall pay each invoice within twenty-one (21) days
of the invoice date. Late payments shall accrue interest
at 1.5% per month from the due date.

5. Source Code Escrow
Developer shall deposit a complete copy of the source code
into escrow within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date,
being by 14 April 2026.

6. Maintenance Term
Following the Warranty Period referenced in Section 1,
Developer shall provide standard maintenance services for
twelve (12) months at the rates set out in Exhibit B.

7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of California.
