STRATEGIC ALLIANCE AND REVENUE PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT

This Strategic Alliance and Revenue Participation Agreement
is entered into between Nexora Capital Group ("Alliance Lead")
and the co-participant identified below ("Participant").

1. Revenue Participation Mechanics
The arrangement contemplated herein shall operate on an
evergreen basis absent an affirmative sunset election by
either party, with any sunset election to take effect at
the close of the second full fiscal quarter following the
quarter in which the electing party's written notification
is actually received and formally acknowledged.

2. Referral Commission Settlement
Alliance Lead shall settle each referral commission within
thirty (30) days of the close of the calendar month in
which the relevant referred transaction achieves final
binding execution by all counterparties.

3. Clawback Mechanics
In the event of a reversed or voided transaction within
the lookback window, the corresponding commission shall
be subject to clawback netting against the next commission
settlement cycle, or separately invoiced if no subsequent
settlement falls within the clawback resolution period.

4. Alliance Continuance Review
The parties shall conduct a continuance review at the end
of each fiscal year to assess whether the strategic
rationale for the alliance remains sufficient to justify
continuation on the existing commercial terms or whether
renegotiation is warranted given prevailing market conditions.

5. Exclusivity Carve-out
The exclusivity obligations herein shall not apply to
any engagement that predates this Agreement's execution
by more than eighteen (18) months or that falls outside
the defined alliance perimeter as documented in Annex A.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
