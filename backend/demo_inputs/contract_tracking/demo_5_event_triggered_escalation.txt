SUPPLIER QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE AGREEMENT

This Supplier Quality and Compliance Agreement is entered into
between Valerian Components Inc. ("Supplier") and the Buyer.

1. Defect Notification
Supplier shall notify Buyer of any confirmed product defect
within forty-eight (48) hours of Supplier becoming aware
of the defect.

2. Corrective Action Plan
Following receipt of a defect notification from Buyer,
Supplier shall submit a corrective action plan within
ten (10) business days of receiving Buyer's written request.

3. Audit Scheduling
Upon receipt of Buyer's written audit request, Supplier
shall confirm audit dates within five (5) business days
of receipt of the request.

4. Recall Response
In the event of a product recall, Supplier shall remove
all affected products from distribution within seven (7)
calendar days of receiving the recall notice from Buyer.

5. Insurance Certificate Renewal
Supplier shall provide Buyer with an updated certificate of
insurance within five (5) business days of each annual
renewal of Supplier's relevant insurance policies.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
